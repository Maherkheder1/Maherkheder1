AdmiBridge Paris Platform

End-to-end engineering and bespoke WordPress architecture for a premier administrative consultancy in Paris, France. Built with custom PHP hooks, tailored Amelia booking workflows, automated transactional email systems, and full trilingual localization (FR / EN / AR).

Live Link

Project Overview & Business Impact

AdmiBridge Paris is a high-trust corporate digital platform designed to streamline legal and administrative consultancy workflows for international clients in France. Rather than relying on off-the-shelf templates, the system was engineered using custom PHP modules, modular shortcodes, and bespoke UI/UX components matching a luxury navy-and-gold brand identity.

The platform automates client onboarding from initial inquiry to confirmed consultation through a deeply customized Amelia Booking pipeline, real-time calendar synchronization, and event-driven HTML email notifications tailored separately for both clients and business administrators.

Key Engineering & WordPress Deliverables

  • Custom PHP & WordPress Hooks Architecture: Developed modular, conflict-free PHP functions, custom shortcodes, and WordPress actions/filters adhering to WP coding standards, security sanitization (esc_url, nonces), and clean separation of concerns.
  • Amelia Booking System Customization: Engineered and styled end-to-end consultation scheduling flows using Amelia, customizing service tiers, staff availability rules, timezone handling, and responsive booking interfaces.
  • Automated Transactional Email Workflows (SMTP & HTML Templates): Programmed dynamic, brand-consistent HTML email notifications triggered on form submissions and appointment events—delivering instant localized confirmations to clients and structured lead/booking alerts to business owners via authenticated SMTP (SPF/DKIM).
  • Trilingual Localization & Bidirectional Engine (FR / EN / AR): Built dynamic locale detection integrating Polylang/WPML and URI routing to automatically switch content, legal compliance pages (CGU, CGV, Mentions Légales), directional icons, and full RTL/LTR CSS layouts.
  • Core Web Vitals, Technical SEO & Security Hardening: Optimized asset delivery, caching compatibility, semantic HTML5 structure, schema markup, and GDPR/RGPD compliance for European regulatory standards.
