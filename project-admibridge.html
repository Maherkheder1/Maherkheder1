AdmiBridge Paris Platform

AdmiBridge Paris is a multilingual corporate platform for an administrative consultancy based in Paris, France. Designed and engineered with a luxury navy-and-gold visual identity, full trilingual support (French, English, Arabic), and seamless RTL/LTR layout switching.

Live Link

Project Overview

AdmiBridge Paris serves international clients and expatriates navigating administrative procedures in France. The website was built from the ground up to reflect high-end corporate trust, offering structured service breakdowns, transparent pricing, appointment booking, and comprehensive legal documentation.

On the technical side, custom PHP components and shortcodes were developed to dynamically detect the active locale (Polylang / WPML / URI routing), automatically adapting content, legal URLs, navigation arrows, and layout direction between LTR (French & English) and RTL (Arabic) with full cross-device responsiveness.
